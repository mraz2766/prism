Prism modifications: converted the original CSV into a compact country boundary
index; adjacent equal-country ranges are coalesced; unknown/unassigned country
codes are retained as unknown. No city, coordinates or ISP data is included.
The index is an adaptation of DB-IP Country Lite, not a DB-IP endorsement of Prism.

The data is provided without warranties. IP-based location is approximate and
may be incomplete or outdated. A country result is not a device location.

Source and generated-file SHA-256 checksums, release month and exact provenance
are recorded in country-dbip.source.json. The database may be updated manually
with scripts/build-country-database.py. Prism never downloads data at runtime.
